Hail of Fire: Update Notes, June 2026

A new version of Hail of Fire is live on WargameVault. Here's what changed and why.

Artillery

Fire Missions (new rule). Each Artillery Unit may now Fire for Effect a limited number of times per game, by weight: 4 Light, 3 Medium, 2 Heavy. Artillery is very effective, as it should be. The limitation is both more practical and realistic. Smoke counts as a mission too.

Smoke. Smoke now scales with weight (3" Light, 4" Medium, 5" Heavy) and lasts as long as the barrage. It follows the full barrage procedure, so you sustain it like HE fire, rolling 4+ at the start of your turn.

Open-Topped vehicles vs barrage. Open-Topped vehicles no longer roll for penetration against barrage RFPs, they simply don't reduce them.

Off-board Forward Observers. An off-board battery's FO now counts as a Platoon Leader for Order Dice. If destroyed, they don't require BP rolls, but they stop giving an Order Die.

Firing

Multi-Weapon Firing. Any Team with multiple weapons may now fire one at full RoF and each other at RoF 1, instead of main gun OR MGs. A Sherman can fire its 75mm and its MGs in the same activation.

Vehicle MGs cleaned up. All Vehicle MGs are now RoF 3. The Open-Topped penalty for firing an AA MG or .50 is gone. Instead, enclosed armored vehicles lose their pintle-mounted MGs.

Troop Quality

Elite and Poor troops re-roll one die on RFPs. When resolving Received Fire Points, Elite Teams now re-roll a single 1 and Poor Teams re-roll a single 6. A lone Kill is usually shrugged off, but two 1s at once still destroy an Elite.

Movement

Terrain stops fewer tanks. Difficult terrain now slows tracked and half-tracked vehicles rather than require a Training Check. Wheeled vehicles still test or stop, and Very Difficult terrain still potentially halts all.

Order Dice

Half-strength vehicle units still function. A Vehicle Unit now generates no command (no Order Die, no leader to nominate) only once more than half its Teams are out of action (destroyed, suppressed, or carrying unresolved RFPs). A unit at exactly half still generates its die.

Hero Points

Hero Points open at 3 minimum. The pre-game Hero Point roll now floors at 3 (a 1 or 2 counts as 3). Mid-game re-rolls of the Hero Die stay as swingy.

Company Commander

Field Promotion (new ability). A Company Commander within 4" of a leaderless Unit may now place a new PL Team in base contact during his activation, no Training Check. He may still move and act normally afterward.

The Basic Scenario

Deployment and objectives (tightened). Balance the forces first (Force Parity Procedure or the Arsenal's Quick Compare), and the weaker force chooses to Attack or Defend. The Attacker places all objectives, scaled to the Defender (one, plus one per two Defender Units), before the Defender picks an edge. The end-of-turn Break Point rolls are gone; taking an objective off the enemy now generates 2 rolls instead.

Hidden Teams reveal restriction. Hidden Teams from the same Unit no longer have to reveal within 12" of each other.

Open-topped vehicles spot like infantry. Open-topped vehicles now reveal Hidden Teams at 16" like infantry. Recon Units spot at 24" (up from 20").

Force Parity Procedure

Handicap is now a Break Point penalty. Instead of reducing its Break Limit, the stronger force now starts with a Break Point penalty equal to the score difference. This matches how Quick Compare hands out its handicap.

Heavy Hitters bonus (new). A pairing scores an extra point if the winning unit: more Teams with AT 6+, a front-and-flank armor sum of 6+, or FP 3 than its pair, or 10+ Infantry/Gun Teams.

Unclear pairings. If it's not clear which unit is scarier, skip the pairing rather than handing it to the defender.

Special Rules

Unreliable (rewritten). If any movement die shows a 1, roll 1d6 per Unreliable Team in the Unit; a roll of 1 suppresses that Team.

Anti-Aircraft guns fire 360°. AA guns now fire in any direction at ground targets, not just their forward arc, reflecting their rotating mounts.

Optional Rules

Snipers (now a Unit type). Snipers are now a Unit of one Small Team rather than a special Team that costs an Order Point to reveal. They generate no Order Dice and don't add to the Break Limit.

Stat Adjustments

US M3 37mm Gun. Range: 20" to 16"

Soviet IS-2. Gun range: 20" to 24"

Hail of Fire is free. If you're getting value from it, bring it to the table, tell someone, or join us on Discord. Download the latest on WargameVault.

Thanks again for playing.
