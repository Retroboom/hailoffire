The Turn & The Clock

Air step (Order Dice, before Order Points): resolve aircraft arriving/leaving. Then alternate activations.

No turn limit. The game ends when a side meets its victory objective, a force's hidden Break total tops its Break Limit (that force loses), or the board empties (draw).

Break Limit = 5 + 2 per Unit, rolled and hidden.

He is leaving. The enemy does not need to win, only to still exist tomorrow. Every man who walks out is one you can no longer reach.

Escalation Track

US fire support unlocks as the battle escalates. Clock starts the first turn a Team Fires or is revealed. At the end of each US turn, roll 1d6, add to the Total.

Urgent request - spend 2 Hero Points to roll a 2nd Escalation die. Once/turn.

A call within an asset's Danger Close of any friendly Team needs a Training Check; fail refuses it (OP spent, no Fire Mission used). Barrage hits everyone in radius.

The gunship is the close weapon: at 8" it is the only thing callable on an enemy on top of you.

Concealment (PAVN / NLF)

  • Total Hidden - all Units deploy Hidden, anywhere in zone.
  • Spider Holes - Hidden Teams count Dug-In (Hard Cover) until they Move.
  • Fade Away - a Team that starts & ends its activation out of LoS of all enemy (landed birds included) and outside reveal distance re-hides. If seen at the start, no fade.

Kill Zone (simplified)

A Team that reveals within 4" of an enemy and Fires that activation gets +1 RoF and the target may not claim Concealment.

Reveal distances

Hidden teams reveal ≥16" from you (8" vs vehicles, 24" Recon), measured from where you began your move. Out of your LoS before the move? They reveal at any range. Move far and the ambush opens close.

Helicopters · AA Tiers

Choppers cost no Order Point, not part of a platoon. Engaged from the air only when entering, leaving, or loitering. On the ground: normal fire.

Anti-Air = DShK 12.7, ZPU-2. Core AA: RoF dice vs 1d6; higher = hit; save vs gun's AT; any fail = driven off.

A helicopter is never Killed by RFPs (a Killed result becomes Suppressed). Only AT penetration removes it.

The Slick, Step by Step

  1. Commit (air step): place LZ, open ground, 8" from revealed enemy. No OP.
  2. Entry AA: Anti-Air Teams that didn't activate last turn intercept.
  3. Door guns may fire (below).
  4. Unload: troops disembark within 2" but are inert this turn (no Move, no Fire).
  5. On the ground: AR 0 vehicle. Enemy fires it on their activations or via a Hero Point. Small arms = RFPs (cap Suppressed); AT = dead with all aboard.
  6. Depart (next air step): resolve RFPs (Killed → Suppressed), exit AA window; fly off or loiter Airborne.

Door guns

Twin M60s as one MG: 16", RoF 3, RFPs only. Fire once on arrival, once on departure; never parked.

Losing birds

Any helo destroyed: owner rolls 1 Break Point (2 for a Dust-Off).

Triage & Dust-Off

  • Wound Roll - a US non-Vehicle Team taking a Killed from Fire (not assault) rolls 1d6: 5+ destroyed, else Wounded (marker).
  • Fighting Wounded - a Team carrying a Wound marker fires at RoF 1 and may not assault. 2nd wound or another Killed = destroyed.
  • Carry Him - at start of a Move, pass a Wound marker to an unwounded Infantry Team within 2" (or a Transport). The giver is restored.
  • Medic (1 Small Team/company) - holds up to 3 wounds, never Fires/assaults, no penalty.
  • Dust-Off - always available, never runs out. A carrying Team within 4" loads its wounds aboard when that Team activates. Safe exit Evacuates them.
  • Stakes - a downed Dust-Off = 2 Break rolls, and every wound aboard is lost.

Exfiltration

From the situation's turn (default T4):

  • Revealed Teams exit by moving off a PAVN/NLF edge.
  • Hidden Units exfiltrate by declaration: 1 OP at start of turn to remove a Unit that is all-Hidden (faded the prior turn).

Exited/exfiltrated Teams are not lost for Morale or Break. A Unit fully off-table generates no more Break Points.

Political cost (US) - add +1 to every Break Point die you roll against your own force.
