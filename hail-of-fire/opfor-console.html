03 Contacts

Each Contact is a place the OpFor might be. Trip one when a Reveal Trigger fires (2.6). The console knows which are Nerves. You do not.

04 Hero Points

The OpFor's pool is hidden and spent by the triggers in 2.5. Tell the console what you are doing; it answers.

05 The Oracle

Fires on a Hero Die 6 or 1. Roll it by hand any time you want chaos.

06 OpFor Roster

A unit that is Shaken contributes no Order Die: a Platoon Leader with unresolved RFPs, or a Vehicle Unit with more than half its Teams destroyed, suppressed, or carrying RFPs.
